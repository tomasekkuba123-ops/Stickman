Stickman's bed just woke up... and it wanted to play!
It bounced him out of his blankets and onto the ceiling.
Then it galloped down the stairs like a puppy!
Stickman chased it all the way to the playground.
The bed jumped on a trampoline... higher and HIGHER!
So Stickman drew a giant pillow with his magic pencil.
The bed flopped down on it... and let out a huge yawn.
Stickman tucked it in, and they both fell fast asleep.
Sweet dreams... until his toothbrush started to sing.
