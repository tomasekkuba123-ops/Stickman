# EP10 ledger: "What If Gravity Disappeared for 10 Seconds?"
- First episode with a script written directly by Claude (no user-supplied script/voiceover text this round) — topic chosen to differ from prior episodes (moon/dragon/money -> gravity), reusing the existing character/voice/motion/environment libraries only.
- Voice: Kokoro am_michael, 39.80 s (natural length, under the 40 s cap); Whisper word timing; Rhubarb mouths; -14 LUFS mix with synthesized SFX (hum/riser/pop/glass/click/whoosh/thud2/heart/buzz/swing/impact/chime/sparkle/slam/thud/rumble/click) and a procedural music bed (playful -> quirky -> tense -> soar -> tense -> playful).
- One fixed spot (SeasonHill, autumn, reused as-is from EP09's kit) — no new environment built. New per-episode assets: a floating mug + phone (simple canvas-drawn billboards) and a handful of drifting leaf sprites.
- `floatY(t)` lifts the hero's own world position (not the action library's "float" action, which conflicts with the `gest` speech-gesture system); a static, ground-anchored camera for the weightless/crash shots (deliberately NOT tracking his rise) so the float and the crash both read clearly against a fixed frame, with extra headroom at the float's peak and a camera-shake + flash/dust effect on landing.
- QC (qc_check.py): PASS. 1194 frames, 39.8 s; visible 100 %; big enough 87.9 %; 40-60 % band 78.2 %; fully in frame 100 %; no caption on face; head white 99.6 %; -14.1 LUFS / -1.2 dBTP; 6 cuts, mean shot 5.67 s (warn: one 12.7 s continuous weightless/crash stretch; warn: hands over captions 56 % of caption time, advisory per the EP09 v18f policy).
- Fixed two real tooling bugs found by this episode (first non-integer-second episode length): `.claude/hooks/post_render.sh` rounded the measured duration to a whole second before passing it to qc_check.py (false FAIL at 39.8 s -> "expected 40.0 s"); `out/ep10v2/cuts.txt` needed every real scale-changing shot boundary listed, not just the hook/ending ones, to stop qc_check.py's V-scale check flagging intentional wider/closer shots as in-shot jumps.
- First build (v1) passed automated QC but a fresh-eyes review caught that the camera fix for a head-crop bug had made it track the hero's rise 1:1, hiding the float/crash beats entirely; rebuilt (v2) with a static camera instead. Verified directly against full-resolution frames from the final render (not just the QC contact sheet, which is too small to reliably show a subtle rise) before delivery.
- Render spanned several 2 h background-task restarts (resumed automatically each time via final4k.sh's per-part skip-if-exists logic) across both the v1 and v2 builds.
