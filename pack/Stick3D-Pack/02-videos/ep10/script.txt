Stickman's shadow just STOLE his magic pencil!
Then it drew a black doorway in the street.
And the entire town started losing its colors!
Stickman chased it past the market. Too slow!
The shadow climbed a wall and swallowed the last bit of daylight!
Then Stickman spotted a bucket of white paint.
He slid, grabbed it, and SPLASH! The shadow turned white!
The pencil fell. He erased the doorway, and colors came rushing back.
Problem solved... until his hoodie winked at him.
