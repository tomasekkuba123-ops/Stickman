What if gravity disappeared, just for ten seconds?
Everything not strapped down would float: your drink, your phone, even you.
Leaves would lift off the trees, your backpack would float beside you, and anything loose in the yard would drift upward.
Your blood would shift toward your head, so you'd feel dizzy, and your stomach would flip like a drop on a roller coaster.
Your hair and hoodie strings would float straight up, and for a few seconds you'd feel completely weightless.
Then, just as fast as it left, gravity would snap back, and everything would crash down at once.
So... what if gravity vanished for ten seconds? Would you survive the landing?
