What if Stickman drew a million dollars?
One bill. Then another. Then a whole stack, taller than him.
He bought a pizza. Then the pizza shop. Then the whole street.
He drew a gold car, a gold house, and a gold toilet. Just because he could.
But people noticed.
His neighbor borrowed a pencil. Then the mailman. Then the whole town.
By lunchtime, everyone was a millionaire.
By dinner, a pizza cost three million dollars.
By midnight, people were using money as blankets.
Stickman looked at his giant pile of cash. It was worth… nothing.
He needed something nobody could draw.
He thought. And thought. And then he drew a tiny seed.
He planted it, watered it, and waited.
Everyone laughed. Until morning.
A tree. Full of real, juicy apples.
Suddenly the whole town wanted to trade.
A gold car for one apple. A gold house for two.
Stickman smiled, took a bite… and the apple tasted like pencil.
What would YOU draw if money was worthless?
