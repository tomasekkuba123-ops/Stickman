What if the Moon vanished tonight?
First, you'd notice the dark. No moonlight, just stars, and the nights get much, much darker.
Then the oceans change. The Moon causes most of our tides, so they'd shrink to less than half.
Animals that hunt, migrate, and even spawn by moonlight would get confused. Whole ecosystems would be shaken.
And over millions of years, Earth's tilt could start to wobble, making the seasons wild and unpredictable.
So... what if the Moon vanished tonight? Would you even notice?
