What if Stickman lived in the Stone Age?
He woke up inside a freezing cave.
Outside, a giant mammoth was blocking his path.
Stickman offered him a delicious apple.
They quickly became best friends.
But walking everywhere was exhausting.
So Stickman invented the very first wheel.
Then he built a wooden car.
The mammoth jumped in, and they raced downhill.
Everything was perfect... until they invented the first traffic jam!
