Stickman typed in the year 2026… but his finger slipped.
Two zero two six became two zero two... SIX MILLION.
The time machine shook, sparked, and vanished.
When the smoke cleared, there was no city. No trees. No people.
Just giant glowing jellyfish floating over a purple ocean.
One of them looked at Stickman… and waved.
It spoke in bubbles. Blub. Blub-blub.
Stickman waved back. Big mistake.
In this future, waving meant: I challenge you.
A thousand jellyfish lit up at once.
Stickman jumped back into the machine and smashed every button.
Wrong button. Wrong button. Right button!
Whoosh. He landed in his room, breathing hard.
He wiped his forehead, sat down… and typed in the year 2026.
But his finger slipped.
Future or past? Where would you go?
