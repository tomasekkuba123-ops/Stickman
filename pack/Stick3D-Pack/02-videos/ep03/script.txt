What if Stickman became a wizard?
One night, he discovered a mysterious magic hat.
Suddenly, a glowing star fell from the sky.
The little star was lost and wanted to go home.
Stickman waved his magic wand, but nothing happened.
So he drew a magical rocket with his pencil.
Together, they blasted into the night sky.
Stickman gently returned the star to its place.
The entire universe lit up with beautiful colors.
But then… another hundred stars fell from the sky!
