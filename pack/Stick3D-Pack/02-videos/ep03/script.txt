What if Stickman drew a rocket… and it actually launched?
He sketched one fin, two fins, a round little window.
The paper shook. Smoke poured from the bottom.
Three. Two. One.
The rocket tore off the page and Stickman grabbed the ladder just in time.
Clouds flashed past.
The sky turned dark blue, then black, then full of stars.
The engine coughed. Fuel: empty.
Stickman panicked, then remembered the pencil in his pocket.
He drew a gas station. On the Moon.
A tiny robot rolled out, filled the tank, and handed him a receipt.
Thirty million dollars.
Stickman drew a coin.
The robot did not look happy.
So he drew a bigger coin.
It fell, rolled across a crater, and woke up something enormous sleeping under the dust.
Two glowing eyes opened.
A giant space whale yawned, and swallowed the rocket whole.
Inside it was warm, quiet, and full of lost satellites.
Stickman smiled and picked up his pencil.
What would you draw to get out?
