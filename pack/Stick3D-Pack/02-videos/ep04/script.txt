What if Stickman erased gravity?
He took his pencil, flipped it around, and rubbed out one tiny word on the page: GRAVITY.
For a second, nothing happened.
Then his sock floated off the floor.
Then his bed.
Then the whole house lifted off its foundations.
Cars drifted down the street like balloons.
A cow floated past the window, still chewing.
Stickman laughed, kicked off the ceiling, and flew.
But the ocean was rising too.
Giant blobs of water wobbled into the sky, full of confused fish.
And the Moon was getting closer. Much closer.
Stickman panicked and tried to write GRAVITY back.
But the pencil floated out of his hand.
He swam after it through the air, past a shark in a water bubble,
grabbed it, and wrote one big word across the sky.
Everything slammed back down.
The house. The cars. The cow.
And one very surprised shark, sitting on his roof.
What would you erase?
